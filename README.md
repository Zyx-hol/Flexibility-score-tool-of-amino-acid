# Flexibility-score-tool-of-amino-acid
A tool which can score residues in protein based on R.
Reference does not rely on three-dimensional structural information and is widely used in biomolecular structure analysis, prediction of disordered regions, and structural and functional studies of Karplus-Schulz amino acid flexibility evaluation systems. The 20 standard amino acids were divided into rigid (A, L, H, V, Y, I, F, C, W, M) and flexible (K, s, G, P, D, E, Q, T, N, R) , combined with the normalized backbone temperature factor (B-factor) parameters (BNORM0/BNORM1/BNORM2) in the high-resolution X-ray crystal structure database, a sliding window weighting algorithm (weight coefficient: 0.25,0.50,0.75,1.00,0.75,0.50,0.25) was adopted, and the results were compared with the experimental data, the flexibility value of each amino acid in the sequence was calculated bit by bit. However, the classical methods have limitations: the lack of neighborhood information at the head and tail of the sequence leads to the deviation of flexibility prediction; there is a lack of quantitative evaluation of the overall flexibility level of the sequence. In this project, an interactive analysis tool was developed to eliminate the local flexibility value of three amino acids at the beginning and end of the sequence, and eliminate the interference of boundary effect, the arithmetic mean of the flexibility values of the effective residues (4th to n-3rd positions for N ≥7) is introduced.

# 基于R语言的氨基酸柔性评价工具
参考不依赖三维结构信息，被广泛应用于蛋白质序列分析、无序区域预测以及结构功能研究中的Karplus-Schulz氨基酸柔性评价体系。将20种标准氨基酸划分为刚性（A，L，H，V，Y，I，F，C，W，M）和柔性（K，S，G，P，D，E，Q，T，N，R）两种，通过统计目标残基两侧刚性氨基酸的邻接数目（0/1/2），结合高分辨率X射线晶体结构数据库中的归一化主链温度因子（B-factor）参数（BNORM0/BNORM1/BNORM2），采用滑动窗口加权算法（权重系数：0.25，0.50，0.75，1.00，0.75，0.50，0.25），逐位计算序列中各氨基酸的柔性值。经典方法存在局限性：序列首尾端残基因邻域信息的缺失导致柔性预测存在偏差；缺乏对序列整体柔性水平的量化评估。本项目编写交互式分析工具，剔除序列首尾各3个氨基酸的局部柔性值，消除边界效应干扰，引入有效残基（N≥7时，第4至N-3位）柔性值的算术均值。

Citation：Karplus PA, Schulz GE. Prediction of chain flexibility in proteins[J]. Naturwissenschaften. 1985, 72(4):212-213.
